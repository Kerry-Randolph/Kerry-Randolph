# hi, i'm kerry

software developer and musician in anchorage, alaska. around town i go by kash bo.

i'm building **that's what's up!**, a free app for locals. point your phone at a flyer on a community bulletin board, and the event goes straight into your calendar or off to a friend. every flyer scanned joins a shared list of what's on around town. coming soon to google play.

**what i work with:** flutter and dart, go, postgresql and postgis, next.js, aws.

**open to work:** freelance and contract projects, especially mobile apps and the backends behind them.
